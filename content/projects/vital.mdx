**Vital** is a healthcare ecosystem built at **INCUBE**. From a single web or mobile app, patients book medical appointments, access lab results and diagnoses, and manage insurance & dependents — across a network of recognized clinical brands in the Dominican Republic.

It ships as four coordinated surfaces sharing the same backend and brand:

- **vital.do** — public marketing site
- **app.vital.do** — patient web app (login, appointments, results)
- **iOS** app (App Store, requires iOS 15.1+)
- **Android** app (Google Play)

## What users can do

- Book medical appointments online, in minutes
- Access medical test results and diagnoses securely
- Get appointment reminders
- Manage dependents and insurance policies from a single profile
- Cancel or reschedule appointments anytime
- Browse a centralized medical history

## Clinical network

Vital aggregates several specialty brands under one roof:

- **Pediatrix** — pediatrics
- **Punto Médico** — general & specialist medical care
- **Punto Visual** — vision
- **Salud Bucal** — dental

## By the numbers

**30,000+** people are part of the Vital ecosystem today.

## Stack

- **Web** (vital.do, app.vital.do): Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Mobile**: native iOS and Android, shipped through the App Store and Google Play
- **Backend**: shared services with INCUBE's wider platform (MongoDB, NextAuth.js, REST APIs)
- **Delivery**: Docker + Kubernetes, GitLab CI/CD

## Latest release

**v1.1.0** (May 12, 2026) shipped a redesigned appointment detail screen, a cleaner cancellation flow, reorganized profile, editable dependents and a new insurance policy details view.
